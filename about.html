About BOYD Automotive

Independent, family-run, and still doing things the way we always have — properly.

From one ramp to a full workshop and forecourt

BOYD Automotive started in 1998 with a single ramp and a promise: fair prices, honest advice, and work done properly first time. Nearly three decades on, we've grown into a full workshop and forecourt — but the promise hasn't changed.

We're not part of a franchise and we don't answer to head office. That means when we tell you a job needs doing, it's because it needs doing — not because it hits a monthly target. It also means every car on our forecourt has been through the same workshop that services it, so we know exactly what we're selling.

Today BOYD Automotive is run day-to-day by our small team of qualified technicians and advisors, most of whom have been with us for over a decade.
