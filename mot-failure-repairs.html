FAQ

Common questions

Can you repair a car that failed its MOT somewhere else?

Yes. You can bring your MOT failure list to BOYD Automotive and we can carry out the required repairs.

Can you check what failed on my MOT?

Yes. Send us your MOT failure information via WhatsApp and we'll advise you on the next step.

Do I need to have the MOT re-test at the same garage?

Not necessarily. The requirements depend on where and when the re-test is carried out.

How much will MOT failure repairs cost?

It depends on the faults identified during the MOT. We can inspect the vehicle and explain the required repairs before work starts.

Can you repair brakes and suspension after an MOT failure?

Yes. We carry out brake, suspension, steering and other common MOT-related repairs.
